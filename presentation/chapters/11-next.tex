  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Version 7.1.0 is live, published \NPublished. Two layers, both so that IMKG's own
      questions can be asked.
    \item The meme's own image: IMKG used Google Vision on it; MemeAtlas reads it with the same vision
      model as the templates (two days of the lab's GPU for \NFrameImages\ pictures) and links what it

    \item Wikidata's facts: \NStatements\ statements of \NStatementItems\ linked items, \NStatementProps\
      properties, from the same Wikidata dump as the lexicon. ``Which memes show a human?'' is now a
      query.

      from the same queries on 7.1.0. They did not run on 7.0.0.
    \item SpongeBob: \NQSponge\ memes show him in their own picture, \NQSpongeTemplates\ through their
      templates. Where Google Vision said ``SpongeBob'', the model names who is actually there:
      Mr.\ Krabs, Squidward.
    \item People: Trump first in both. IMKG's second, Kyle Craven, is Bad Luck Brian --- Google
      Vision matched his photo to the pages naming him; our model reads it as ``man''.
    \item Films: \NQFilms\ entries about \NQFilmItems\ films (Avengers: Endgame first) against 413 ---
      twice the entries.
    \item Gender: fewer people, the same shape (about three men to one woman). We import facts one
      step out from what memes link to; IMKG also imported around each meme's own Wikidata item.
  \end{itemize}}
