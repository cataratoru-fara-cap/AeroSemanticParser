\gmgo{picture-doge}{Doge's templates}
\begin{frame}{Doge, step 5: the blank pictures people caption}
  \centering
  \resizebox{!}{5.5cm}{\input{figures/generated/fig-picture-doge}}\par\vskip3mm
  {\normalsize\color{gmInk2}imgflip, searched for ``doge'': \NDogeCandidates\ candidates, copies merged, \textbf{\color{gmInk}\NDogeTemplates\ kept}, most varied first}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Layman version: a meme is usually a blank picture people write their own caption on.
      imgflip is the website where people make them; it calls the blank pictures templates.
    \item For Doge we searched imgflip for ``doge'': 120 candidate pictures. Many are the same
      picture uploaded again --- resized, re-compressed, mirrored --- so copies are merged, and the
      eight most relevant and most different are kept.
    \item The first one is certain: Doge's own KYM page links to it (``Meme Generator''). The
      others are scored: the name, the picture, the rank on imgflip.
    \item Notice the family: Swole Doge vs.\ Cheems, Dogecoin --- the graph will connect them.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item A vision model (qwen3-vl:32b, on the lab's GPUs) looks at each template and returns
      boxes: what is where, of what kind, and its name if it is someone or something specific.
    \item Left: it finds Doge, and Cheems --- whose Wikidata item is Balltze, the real Shiba Inu
      behind Cheems. Right: Elon Musk holding Doge, the Dogecoin template.
    \item The names are linked to Wikidata by the same linker as the text (chapter 4) --- this is
      why linking comes before the pictures: the linker prefers the items the page's own text
      already linked. The orange boxes are linked items: IMKG's ``its picture shows''
      (m4s:fromImage), for templates.
    \item Checked and audited blind: every box and name is validated before it is stored; a sample
      is read again without the entry's text, and 85\% of the names come back the same --- the
      title did not put names in the model's mouth.
    \item Only named things, printed text that names something, and the three largest ordinary
      regions reach the graph: ``a man'' is in 6,800 templates and says nothing.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

\gmgo{picture-search}{Finding templates}
\begin{frame}{\NTemplates\ templates, found politely}
  \centering
  \begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=1cm,
      st/.style={circle, minimum size=1.15cm, fill=gm@col@picture, text=white, inner sep=0pt, font=\fontsize{15}{15}\selectfont},
      nm/.style={font=\normalsize\bfseries, text=gmInk, align=center},
      what/.style={text width=2.7cm, align=center, font=\small, text=gmInk2},
      go/.style={-{Stealth[length=3mm]}, line width=1.4pt, draw=gmBase, shorten <=1.5mm, shorten >=1.5mm}]
    \foreach \i/\ic/\n/\w in {0/\faSearch/search/{imgflip's public pages, for each title},
                              1/\faSortAmountDown/score/{the name, the picture, the rank},
                              2/\faClone/merge/{copies: the same picture, resized or mirrored},
                              3/\faRandom/vary/{most varied first; 0, or 1 to 10},
                              4/\faEye/read/{the vision model, every template}} {
      \node[st] (s\i) at (\i*2.95,0) {\ic};
      \node[nm, below=1.5mm of s\i] (n\i) {\n};
      \node[what, below=0mm of n\i] {\w};}
    \foreach \i [count=\j] in {0,...,3} \draw[go] (s\i) -- (s\j);
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \vskip3mm
  \begin{columns}[t]
    \column{.24\textwidth}\centering\gmbig{\NFramesSelected}{entries with one}
    \column{.24\textwidth}\centering\gmbig{\NCandidates}{candidates seen}
    \column{.24\textwidth}\centering\gmbig{\NRegions}{things seen}
    \column{.24\textwidth}\centering\gmbig{\FTplPrecision}{precision}
  \end{columns}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item IMKG linked templates to 96 entries, by hand and by title similarity. MemeAtlas searches
      imgflip for every meme's title (\NFramesSearched\ entries) and scores each candidate: name similarity,
      the picture compared with the entry's own images, the search rank, imgflip's own pick.
    \item KYM's own link is the answer key: with it hidden, the search still finds the linked
      template in the top 10 for 78.7\% of entries (top 1: 51.3\%).
    \item ``The same picture'' is a perceptual fingerprint: resized, re-encoded, mirrored and
      letterboxed copies merge (\NMerged\ merged); crops and edits stay apart --- a known limit.
    \item Most varied first: ten templates are not ten copies of one picture.
    \item \NFramesSelected\ entries got templates (\NFramesSelectedPct\%): \NNoResults\ had no imgflip result --- mostly
      entries that are not picture memes --- and \NBelowThreshold\ only weak candidates. \NTemplates\ templates
      kept; \NRead\ read by the vision model, \NReadFailed\ failures; \NRegions\ regions, \NNamed\ named. Precision about 0.95 on
      contact sheets (read by Claude, not yet by a person --- say it).
    \item Politeness is a design constraint: imgflip's terms allow only its public pages, so about
      one request a second, never its internal search API, which would be 40 times faster.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
