  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item IMKG: the first knowledge graph of Internet memes, published at ESWC 2023 by Riccardo
      Tommasini (here at LIRIS), Filip Ilievski and Thilini Wijesiriwardene.
    \item Three things existed: the paper, the code on GitHub, and one data release on Zenodo.
    \item Sources: Know Your Meme (the encyclopedia of memes), imgflip (the meme generator), and
      Wikidata. 12,585 KYM entries, 1.3 million imgflip memes, 4.85 million nodes.
    \item Most of the size is imgflip: 15 of the 16.5 million edges are imgflip memes and their
      captions. The KYM part is 167,662 nodes and 914,941 edges.
  \end{itemize}}

  \begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=1cm,
      stp/.style={circle, minimum size=15mm, draw=gm@col@before, line width=1.4pt, fill=gmSurface,
                   font=\small\bfseries, text=gmInk},
      what/.style={text width=3.1cm, align=center, font=\scriptsize, text=gmInk2},
      go/.style={-{Stealth[length=2.4mm]}, line width=1pt, draw=gmBase, shorten <=1mm, shorten >=1mm}]
    \node[stp] (m) at (0,0) {model};
    \node[stp] (c) at (3.7,0) {collect};
    \node[stp] (e) at (7.4,0) {enrich};
    \node[stp] (i) at (11.1,0) {integrate};
    \draw[go] (m) -- (c); \draw[go] (c) -- (e); \draw[go] (e) -- (i);
    \node[what, below=3mm of m] {media frames, memes, templates};
    \node[what, below=3mm of c] {a crawler over Know Your Meme (two days), imgflip, \FImkgSeeds{} Wikidata memes};
    \node[what, below=3mm of e] {DBpedia Spotlight on the text, Google Vision on the image, Wikidata via KGTK};
    \node[what, below=3mm of i] {RML to RDF; \FImkgPsixseven{} memes and \FImkgLinkedFrames{} templates joined by hand};
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \vskip8mm
  {\large\color{gmInk2}Four steps, run once, by hand --- a \textbf{\color{gmInk}snapshot}.}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item IMKG's four steps (Fig.~4 of the paper): design the model, collect, enrich, integrate.
    \item Collect: a Selenium crawler over all of Know Your Meme, about two days; imgflip's memes;
      Wikidata's 556 memes as seeds.
    \item Enrich: entities from the text with DBpedia Spotlight (a web API, confidence 0.5), from
      the image with Google's Vision API (paid, so only one image per meme), background facts
      from Wikidata with KGTK.
    \item Integrate: RML mappings to RDF; KYM and Wikidata joined on 276 memes; KYM and imgflip
      joined by hand and by title matching (about 60 matches above 85\%).
    \item Then it was published. Nothing re-ran it.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What I found}
  \begin{columns}[t]
    \column{.5\textwidth}
      \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{3.2mm}
        \item \textbf{A snapshot}, not a process: Know Your Meme grows every day
        \item \textbf{Remote, paid services} in the middle: not repeatable
        \item \textbf{Most of each page unread}: links, references, the story
        \item \textbf{Bridges built by hand}: \FImkgPsixseven{} memes, \FImkgLinkedFrames{} frames
        \item \textbf{No evidence on a link}: no score, no reason
      \end{itemize}
    \column{.44\textwidth}
      \vskip4mm
      \begin{tikzpicture}[baseline=(current bounding box.north)]
        \node[fill=gmTint, rounded corners=2mm, inner sep=4mm, text width=5.2cm, align=left] {
          {\Large\color{gm@col@before}``}{\itshape we plan recurrent releases of the KGs [\dots] to handle
          scalability in terms of volume and velocity''}\par\vskip2mm
          {\scriptsize\color{gmMuted}IMKG, ESWC 2023 --- Conclusion and Outlook}};
      \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{columns}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Be fair: IMKG was a research prototype and did what a paper needs. These are the
      things a \emph{living} graph needs, which is what I was asked to build.
    \item Snapshot: KYM adds and edits entries all the time; a one-off crawl is out of date the
      next week.
    \item Remote services: Spotlight and Google Vision answer differently over time, cost money,
      and can disappear --- you cannot rebuild the same graph twice.
    \item Unread: IMKG kept About/Origin/Spread as text and annotated it; every link, every
      reference, every event in the story was left on the page.
    \item Hand-made bridges do not scale to new entries.
    \item And the authors said it themselves: recurrent releases, volume and velocity. That is
      the brief I took.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What I set out to build}
  \vskip2mm
  \begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=1cm, card/.style={rounded corners=2mm, fill=gmTint, inner sep=3.5mm,
                      text width=3.75cm, minimum height=3.3cm, align=left, anchor=north west}]
    \node[card] at (0,0) {{\color{gm@col@before}\Large\faSync}\par\vskip2mm
      {\bfseries A pipeline, not a snapshot}\par\vskip1mm
      {\small\color{gmInk2}runs every month, redoes only what changed, rebuilds the same graph from the same inputs}};
    \node[card] at (4.85,0) {{\color{gm@col@before}\Large\faSearch}\par\vskip2mm
      {\bfseries Every page, read in depth}\par\vskip1mm
      {\small\color{gmInk2}fields, links, references, images, the story --- and how sure we are of each}};
    \node[card] at (9.7,0) {{\color{gm@col@before}\Large\faHandshake}\par\vskip2mm
      {\bfseries Compatible with IMKG}\par\vskip1mm
      {\small\color{gmInk2}its vocabulary kept word for word, so its queries still run}};
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Three goals, which come back in every chapter.
    \item One: a pipeline. Re-runnable, incremental, reproducible: same inputs, same graph.
    \item Two: depth. Read everything on a page, and record how sure we are of every derived fact.
    \item Three: stay compatible with IMKG --- MemeAtlas \emph{extends} IMKG, it does not replace
      it. Where IMKG has a word for something, I use that word.
    \item Now let's go back to the dog, and to the first step: getting the pages.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
