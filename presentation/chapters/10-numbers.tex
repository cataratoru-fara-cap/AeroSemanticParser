  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The fair comparison is with the Know Your Meme part of IMKG (its Table 2), the same source:
      entries, entry types, tags, series and links --- the ``core''.
    \item \NFrames\ entries against \FImkgFrames: \NFramesRatio\ times as many.
    \item Edges counted the RDF way (every literal is an edge in RDF; a property graph keeps literals
      on the nodes): the core has \NCoreTE\ against IMKG's \FImkgKymEdges\ --- the same density over
      nearly twice the entries.
    \item And beyond the core, the layers IMKG did not have: events, templates, curated links, and
      from 7.1.0 what each entry's own picture shows and Wikidata's facts --- \NNodes\ nodes and
      \NEdgesShort\ edges in all.
    \item What each entry carries: \NWithWikidataPct\% at least one Wikidata link; \NWithEventsPct\% events
      (nearly all that have a story); \NWithTemplatePct\% a template --- templates exist for picture
      memes, not for people or events; \NWithImagePct\% a Wikidata item in their own picture (new in
      7.1.0); \NWithAllPct\% all four. Only \NWithNone\ entries have none.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

\gmgo{numbers-origins}{Where memes are born}
\begin{frame}{Where memes are born}
  {\normalsize\color{gmInk2}entries per year, by platform of origin --- one scale for all six}\par\vskip2mm
  \centering
  \resizebox{!}{5.45cm}{\input{figures/generated/fig-numbers-origins}}
  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item From the origin field, canonicalised (chapter 7: ``Twitter'', ``twitter.com'', ``X'' are
      one platform), counted per year.
    \item The eras of Internet culture appear by themselves: 4chan peaks in 2010, Tumblr in 2014,
      YouTube and Reddit in 2019, Twitter in 2020 (by far the biggest: 3,821 memes from 2005 to 2025),
      TikTok in 2022 --- from nothing before 2018.
    \item Every panel has the same scale, so the heights compare directly.
    \item Careful with the right side: KYM writes a meme up after it has proved itself, so the last
      years are not complete. Over all entries the peak year is 2019 (1,814 entries).
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}

      same parent. \NSiblingPairs\ pairs --- and only \NSiblingLinked\ of them link to each other on their
      pages.
      Series and links are different relations; the graph keeps both.
    \item The longest chain in the graph goes through Doge: Bonk Cheems $\to$ \dots\ $\to$ Memes,
      seven steps.
  \end{itemize}}
