  \note{
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \NEntries\ entries, all parsed by the same parser version (\NParser). Most are memes (\NMemes),
      then events, subcultures, people, sites, cultures --- KYM's own categories, which become
      IMKG's classes (kym:Meme, kym:Event \dots).
    \item Only \NReadyPct\% have every field, and that is normal: most entries have no region, a quarter
      have no Origin or Spread. Incomplete is a status, not a rejection: the record lists what is
      missing (corpus\_missing) and stays in the graph.
    \item The parser (kym\_parse.py) is a pure function: HTML in, a typed record out --- no
      database, no scheduler inside. That is why it can be tested on saved pages; the repository
      keeps Doge's page as a test fixture.
    \item Every record carries its parser, schema and corpus-policy versions; a new parser version
      re-parses, an unchanged page (same fingerprint) is not re-read.
    \item Pages that are not entries (photo galleries, videos, articles share the addresses) fail
      the schema and go to a separate dead-letter list --- \NParseFailures\ --- so the entries stay clean and
      nothing is lost.
    \item Tried and dropped: grouping pages by layout automatically. An explicit parser with saved
      test pages was simpler to trust and to fix.
  \end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
